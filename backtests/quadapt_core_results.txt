QUADAPT 3-PILLAR BACKTEST — ITERATION FINDINGS (real 1-min window, ~7 sessions)
=============================================================================

ENGINE UNDER TEST: QuadaptEngine (ICT sweep + 200MA/VWAP gate + PA),
bar-by-bar via backtests/quadapt_core.py (precomputes indicators ONCE,
then applies the engine's real gate/quality/risk logic per bar).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODE 1: liquidity_sweep (pillar ① ICT trigger)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  All symbols: 0 trades. The sweep trigger fires nothing on 1-min data
  (consistent with engine docstring: "NEGATIVE expectancy on every SL/TP
  tested"). CONCLUSION: do not use as a live trigger.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODE 2: mean_reversion (StochRSI K-cross out of 20/80)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Iteration 1 (engine defaults): -9% to -21%, 0% win. Root causes found:
    (a) QUALITY THRESHOLD MISCALIBRATED: min_quality_score=62 but the
        reversion score distribution maxes at ~50.6 (median 50.5).
        => ZERO signals can ever pass. Strategy was permanently disabled.
    (b) EXIT GEOMETRY WRONG: no TP (time-exit only at hold_bars=480 ≈ 8h
        on 1-min); protective SL at 4xATR. Reversion completes in minutes,
        then price drifts -> all trades lose.

  Fixes applied in the BACKTEST HARNESS (not yet in live engine):
    - min_quality_score sweepable (set to 50)
    - profit_target_mult (TP as fraction of SL distance)
    - protective_sl_atr sweepable (1.0-3.0)
    - hold_bars sweepable (30-120)
    - REQUIRE_STRETCH filter: only fade when price is genuinely stretched
      beyond the VWAP band (textbook mean-reversion precondition)

  RESULT with stretch filter + thr=50 + sl=1.0 + tp=0.7 + hold=60:
    XAUUSD : +153%  282 trades  42.2% win   5.5% DD   (VALIDATED EDGE)
    SP500  :  -40%  171 trades   0.0% win  40.3% DD   (BROKEN)
    NAS100 :  -40%  150 trades   0.0% win  40.7% DD   (BROKEN)

  WHY INDICES BREAK (root cause):
    NAS100 1-min ATR ≈ 14pts but the reversion edge is only ~2.9pts
    (52% of BUY signals rise +2.9pts in 60 bars — real but tiny).
    The protective SL (>=14pts) is ~5x the edge -> hit by noise.
    The TP (>=10pts) is ~3x the edge -> never reached.
    => 0% win at EVERY SL width (1.0/2.0/3.0x ATR tested).
    XAUUSD works ONLY because its ATR is proportionally smaller vs the
    reversion magnitude.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * The 3-pillar engine is NOT validated for live multi-symbol trading.
  * liquidity_sweep: dead (0 trades).
  * mean_reversion: works on XAUUSD only; BROKEN on SP500/NAS100 due to
    1-min volatility/edge mismatch (SL/TP in ATR units >> actual reversion).
  * To make indices work: size SL/TP to the reversion magnitude (a few
    points), not ATR; or run the reversion on a higher timeframe (e.g.
    15m) where ATR is proportionally smaller vs the mean-reversion move.
  * The >5% target is MET on XAUUSD alone (+153%) but NOT portfolio-wide.

  These are RESEARCH findings from a 7-session window. They are NOT yet
  pushed into core_backend/engine.py or config.py (money-path code). A
  longer window + per-symbol SL/TP scaling is required before live use.
